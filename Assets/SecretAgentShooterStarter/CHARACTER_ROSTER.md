# Character Roster Starter

## Player Character

### Codename: Black Glass

An elite secret agent with a compact tranquilizer pistol, modular spy armor, and a hidden lair beneath an abandoned luxury resort. Smooth, precise, and tactical. The player can customize suit color, armor panels, visor style, and gadget loadout.

Core silhouette:

- Slim tactical suit.
- Long coat or cropped armored jacket.
- Compact sidearm holster.
- Wrist gadget.
- Optional half-mask or smart glasses.

## Femme Fatale Syndicate

The syndicate should feel stylish, dangerous, and organized. Every operative is an adult character with a strong role, outfit language, and combat specialty.

## Enemy Archetypes

### Velvet Grunt

- Rank: Grunt
- Role: Basic rifle/pistol unit
- Look: glossy black tactical bodysuit, colored rank stripe, compact weapon
- Recruit use: lair guard, basic mission companion

### Crimson Sergeant

- Rank: Sergeant
- Role: squad coordinator
- Look: red-trim armor panels, headset, sidearm and shock baton
- Recruit use: improves companion teamwork

### Ghost Lieutenant

- Rank: Lieutenant
- Role: stealth commander
- Look: pale tactical cloak, smoke canisters, silenced pistol
- Recruit use: stealth mission support

### Iron Orchid

- Rank: Special Operative
- Role: tank/bodyguard
- Look: heavy white armor plates over sleek black suit
- Recruit use: absorbs damage and protects player

### Needle Kiss

- Rank: Special Operative
- Role: medic/toxin expert
- Look: medical injector rig, compact dart launcher, silver-green accents
- Recruit use: heals player or improves tranquilizer research

### Lady Cipher

- Rank: Commander
- Role: hacker boss
- Look: dark chrome suit, luminous glasses, floating drone array
- Recruit use: unlocks Intel Center upgrades and disables enemy security

### Baroness Vanta

- Rank: Commander
- Role: final regional boss
- Look: black formal tactical coat, gold insignia, heavy pistol, elite escort
- Recruit use: unlocks commander-level lair perks after a difficult defection arc

- Grunts: red capsule bodies.
- Sergeants: red capsule plus shoulder cubes.
- Lieutenants: purple capsule plus cloak plane.
- Tanks: wider capsule plus armor cubes.
- Commanders: tall gold-accent capsule with larger shoulder silhouette.
