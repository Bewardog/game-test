# Game Design: Secret Agent Shooter

## Core Fantasy

You are a secret agent running a hidden lair while dismantling a glamorous enemy syndicate of adult femme fatale operatives. Missions earn intel, currency, prototype weapons, recruits, and room upgrades. Your lair starts huge but mostly empty, then slowly becomes a living headquarters filled with recruited operatives, research stations, armory displays, training rooms, and mission support.

## Camera And Combat Pillars

- First-person mode is for precise shooting, stealth infiltration, and close inspection.
- Third-person mode is for mobility, awareness, carrying captives, and cinematic spy action.
- The default weapon is a tranquilizer pistol.
- Other weapons spawn in the field like arena-shooter pickups.
- Base upgrades improve which weapons, armor, ammo, gadgets, and mission items can spawn.

## Nonlethal Capture And Recruitment

1. Shoot an enemy with tranquilizer darts until she is knocked out.
2. Interact to carry her with one of two carry styles:
   - Bridal carry: slower but quieter.
   - Over-shoulder carry: faster but more visible/noisy.
3. Bring her to an extraction point.
4. She is debriefed and can become a recruit.
5. Recruits appear in the lair and can be summoned as support during missions.

This keeps the mechanic clean: recruitment happens through spy defection, persuasion, and gameplay progression after extraction.

## Enemy Syndicate Hierarchy

### Grunts

- Most common.
- Low health, low sedation resistance.
- Usually specialize in one basic role.
- Good for early recruitment and lair staffing.

### Sergeants

- Squad leaders.
- Better aim and tactical awareness.
- Can buff nearby grunts.
- Moderate recruitment value.

### Lieutenants

- Level mini-bosses or objective holders.
- Often guard intel, prototype weapons, or terminals.
- Higher sedation resistance.
- Unlock stronger recruit bonuses.

### Special Operatives

- Rare specialists.
- Unique abilities such as cloaking, close-combat counters, shield armor, hacking, sniper overwatch, or medical support.
- High value as companions.

### Commanders

- Main target for many levels.
- Usually tied to a region, facility, or syndicate division.
- Capturing or defeating them advances the campaign.
- Recruited commanders can unlock powerful lair rooms or mission perks.

## Enemy Specializations

- Hand-to-hand: rushes the player, disarms, pressures in third-person.
- Stealth: flanks, vanishes, ambushes from cover.
- Tank: high armor, slow movement, protects commanders.
- Marksman: accurate ranged fire, weak up close.
- Hacker: disables pickups, cameras, doors, or player gadgets.
- Medic: revives or stabilizes allies.

## Mission Objective Types

- Steal intel from a guarded terminal.
- Neutralize all operatives on the map.
- Capture or defeat the commander.
- Steal a prototype weapon.
- Extract a named target.
- Recruit a specific specialization.
- Plant tracking devices.
- Escape after alarm lockdown.

## Secret Lair Rooms

### Armory

- Unlocks better field weapon spawns.
- Adds ammo, armor, gadgets, and prototype gear.
- Displays collected weapons.

### Research Lab

- Improves tranquilizer speed, duration, dart capacity, armor, stealth gadgets, scanning, and companion abilities.
- Unlocks upgrades such as faster-acting darts and improved nonlethal ammo.

### Operative Customization Room

- Lets you assign recruited operatives to roles:
  - Hand-to-hand bodyguard
  - Stealth infiltrator
  - Tank defender
  - Medic support
  - Hacker support
  - Marksman overwatch
- Lets you swap tactical outfits, armor styles, color themes, and mission loadouts.

### Training Room

- Improves recruit stats.
- Unlocks companion commands.
- Raises mission assist effectiveness.

### Intel Center

- Reveals mission objectives, commander weaknesses, hidden pickups, and bonus extraction routes.

### Medical Bay

- Improves player armor recovery and recruit survival.
- Reduces costs after failed missions.

### Vehicle/Garage Bay

- Unlocks new mission insertion options and faster extraction.

## Economy

Players earn currency from:

- Completing primary objectives.
- Completing optional objectives.
- Extracting prototype weapons.
- Recruiting higher-rank operatives.
- Stealth bonuses.
- Nonlethal completion bonuses.

Currency is spent on:

- Room construction.
- Room upgrades.
- Weapon unlocks.
- Research.
- Recruit specialization training.
- Companion deployment slots.

## Prototype Milestone Plan

### Milestone 1: Core Feel

- Player movement.
- First-person/third-person switching.
- Tranquilizer shooting.
- Knockout state.
- Carry and extraction.

### Milestone 2: Mission Loop

- Objective tracker.
- Pickups.
- Currency reward.
- Base terminal upgrades.

### Milestone 3: Living Lair

- Room unlock visuals.
- Recruits walking around.
- Basic companion summon.

### Milestone 4: Syndicate Campaign

- Ranked enemies.
- Commanders.
- Mission map chain.
- Research and customization.
